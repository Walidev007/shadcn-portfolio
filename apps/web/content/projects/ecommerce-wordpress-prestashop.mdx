Before moving into AI, automation, and functional consulting, I spent six years as a web integrator across several agencies (Lagence123, Priximbattable, X3d Group, Infeeny, Fimwy, Ohmyweb), building and maintaining e-commerce sites for their clients.

## Context

Agency clients needed one of three things:

- a new storefront built from scratch
- an existing site evolved with custom features
- an inherited site stabilized after years of technical debt from previous integrators

## Problem

Agency e-commerce work made it hard to:

- **add new features safely** on sites running outdated themes and plugins
- **meet custom requirements** that off-the-shelf plugins didn't cover
- **migrate without downtime** on stores that couldn't afford to break mid-sale
- **fix recurring performance complaints** that directly hurt client conversion rates

## My Role

Web integrator responsible for development, evolution, migration, and maintenance of client e-commerce sites, and the direct point of contact for client technical support.

## Problems Encountered

- Sites inherited from previous integrators often ran on outdated themes and plugins that slowed page loads and made new features risky to add.
- Clients frequently needed custom functionality that off-the-shelf PrestaShop/WordPress plugins didn't cover.
- Technical migrations (theme changes, platform version upgrades, hosting moves) carried real risk of breaking a live storefront mid-sale.
- Performance issues (slow load times, unoptimized images and assets) were a recurring client complaint that directly affected their conversion rates.

## Solutions Built

**Development.** Built and evolved e-commerce sites on WordPress and PrestaShop, including custom themes and features tailored to each client's specific catalog and workflow needs.

**Migration.** Planned and executed technical migrations with staged rollouts to minimize downtime and risk on live stores.

**Performance.** Diagnosed and fixed performance bottlenecks — caching, image/asset optimization, query and plugin audits — to bring load times down.

**Support.** Provided ongoing maintenance and direct client support, fixing issues quickly to keep storefronts running during peak sales periods.

## Business Value Delivered

- **Faster, more stable storefronts** — resolved technical debt inherited from prior integrators.
- **Custom functionality delivered** — features built to match each client's actual catalog and workflow, not generic plugin defaults.
- **Zero-surprise migrations** — staged rollouts kept live stores online through platform and hosting changes.
- **Long-running client relationships** — repeat and ongoing work across multiple agencies over six years.

## Technical Stack & Why Each Tool Was Selected

- **WordPress / PrestaShop** — the platforms themselves weren't a choice I made per project; they were the client/agency-mandated CMS and e-commerce systems I had to work within, so my focus was mastering their theming and plugin architecture rather than picking the stack.
- **Custom PHP theme and plugin development** — used whenever a client's requirement went beyond what an off-the-shelf plugin could do, since heavily customized catalogs and checkout flows are common in e-commerce.
- **Performance optimization techniques (caching, asset/image optimization)** — applied specifically because slow load times were the most common client-reported problem, and e-commerce conversion is directly sensitive to page speed.
