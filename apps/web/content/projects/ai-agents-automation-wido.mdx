At WIDO, I work as a freelance AI & Automation Consultant helping small and mid-sized businesses turn manual, repetitive work into reliable automations and AI agents.

## Context

Most of the businesses I work with run their day-to-day operations across a patchwork of tools, including:

- a CRM or ERP for customer and business data
- one or more SaaS tools for specific functions (invoicing, support, scheduling)
- spreadsheets and email for anything the other tools don't cover

None of these systems talked to each other, and someone on the team spent hours a week manually moving data between them — re-entering leads, triaging requests, or rebuilding reports by hand.

## Problem

The existing way of working made it hard to:

- **document what the actual process was** — steps lived in people's heads, not in any diagram or spec
- **connect existing tools** — CRM, ERP, and SaaS systems had no integration between them
- **trust an AI-driven solution** — teams had no prior experience with AI agents or automation platforms
- **express some workflow logic** — conditional branching, data transformation, and retries went beyond what pure no-code tools could handle

## My Role

Product builder and AI & Automation Consultant, owning each engagement end-to-end: discovery and framing workshops, process mapping, automation/agent design, build and configuration, testing, documentation, and team training.

## Problems Encountered

- Business processes existed only as tribal knowledge, with no diagrams, documented steps, or clear ownership.
- Leads captured in one system never reached the CRM without manual entry, and reports were rebuilt by hand every week.
- Non-technical teams needed a solution they could understand and trust enough to rely on day to day.
- Some workflows needed logic that pure no-code tools couldn't express cleanly.

## Solutions Built

**Discovery.** Ran structured process-mapping and framing workshops with each client to surface the real steps in their workflow and flag which ones were repetitive enough to automate.

**Build.** Designed and configured AI agents and automated workflows connecting the client's CRM, ERP, and SaaS tools via REST APIs, webhooks, and JSON payloads — covering use cases like lead qualification, automated reporting, and document/email triage. Used LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) inside these workflows for tasks that need reasoning or language understanding rather than pure rule-following, and wrote custom Python and JavaScript steps where the no-code platforms' built-in logic wasn't enough.

**Handover.** Tested each automation against real data before going live, then wrote documentation and delivered hands-on training so the client's team could operate and adjust the automation without me.

## Business Value Delivered

- **Hours saved per week** — manual copy-paste and re-entry work removed from day-to-day operations.
- **Documented, visible processes** — automations run unattended but stay understandable to the client's team.
- **Team autonomy** — non-technical staff trained to request changes and extend workflows themselves.
- **Reliable integrations** — CRM, ERP, and SaaS tools now share data automatically instead of manually.

## Technical Stack & Why Each Tool Was Selected

- **n8n / Make** — visual, low-code workflow orchestration. Chosen because they let me build and iterate fast, and because the resulting workflows are readable enough to hand off to a client's team instead of leaving them dependent on me.
- **Power Automate** — used specifically for clients already invested in Microsoft 365, where it integrates natively with Outlook, SharePoint, and Teams without extra connectors.
- **Claude Code, ChatGPT, Gemini, Codex** — LLM engines plugged into the workflows for reasoning, classification, and content-generation steps that rule-based logic can't handle.
- **Python / JavaScript** — for custom logic (data transformation, edge-case handling) that exceeds what the no-code tools' native nodes support.
- **REST APIs, webhooks, JSON** — the integration layer tying together the client's existing CRM/ERP/SaaS stack, chosen because they're the lowest-friction way to connect systems that were never designed to talk to each other.
