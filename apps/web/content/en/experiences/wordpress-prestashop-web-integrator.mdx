## Context

Over six years and six agencies, I worked as a web integrator building and maintaining e-commerce sites for agency clients — SMBs needing new online stores, custom features, or ongoing technical support.

## My Role & Responsibilities

- Built and evolved e-commerce sites on WordPress and PrestaShop.
- Built custom themes and features tailored to each client's store.
- Handled technical migrations and ongoing maintenance.
- Optimized site performance and provided direct client support.

## Team & Environment

Agency integrator role, working across six different agencies over the period (Lagence123, Priximbattable, X3d Group, Infeeny, Fimwy, Ohmyweb), each serving its own portfolio of e-commerce clients.

## Key Projects Delivered

- Built custom PrestaShop and WordPress themes and plugins for features not covered by off-the-shelf solutions.
- Ran technical migrations of existing online stores to updated platforms and themes.

## KPIs & Results

- Delivered faster, more stable online stores through targeted performance optimization (caching, asset and image optimization).
- Maintained long-term client relationships across multiple agencies over six years.

## Challenges

- Legacy themes and plugins slowing sites down, often requiring a rebuild of the theme's core logic rather than a quick patch.
- Recurring technical debt inherited from previous integrators, needing cleanup before new features could be added safely.
- Clients needing fast turnaround on custom features and fixes, balanced against the stability of live, revenue-generating stores.

## Tech Stack & Why Each Tool Was Chosen

- **WordPress & PrestaShop** — the CMS/e-commerce platforms clients standardized on across every agency engagement.
- **Custom PHP theming & plugin development** — used whenever standard features fell short of a client's needs.
- **Performance optimization (caching, asset/image optimization)** — the direct fix for the load-time issues most clients ran into.
