## Context

WIDO is my own freelance practice: I work directly with SMBs and teams running manual, repetitive processes — lead handling, onboarding, reporting — without a clear view of what could actually be automated or where AI agents fit in.

## My Role & Responsibilities

- Map and analyze business processes to identify automatable tasks and the opportunities hidden inside them.
- Run scoping workshops with business teams to formalize functional requirements.
- Design, configure, and deploy business solutions (CRM, ERP, SaaS tools).
- Design and configure AI agents and automations tailored to each client's processes.
- Integrate and connect tools via APIs and automation platforms.
- Test, harden, and continuously improve automations before they ship to production.
- Document processes and support end users through adoption of new tools.
- Deliver professional training on AI, automation, and new ways of working.

## Team & Environment

Solo freelance engagement model: I work directly with each client's operating team (sales, ops, or founders), acting as the sole technical and functional point of contact from discovery through build to deployment.

## Challenges

- Client processes are rarely documented — most of the discovery work is reconstructing the real process from how people actually work, not from an existing spec.
- Tool sprawl: clients typically run several disconnected CRM/ERP/SaaS tools, so every project starts with an audit of the existing setup before any automation.
- Adoption, more than technology, is usually the harder problem — teams unfamiliar with AI agents or automation platforms need concrete training and a clear case for the value of change (see the proof-of-concept approach on Flowchems).

## Tech Stack & Why Each Tool Was Chosen

- **n8n / Make** — visual, low-code workflow orchestration; fast to build and easy to hand off to non-developer teams.
- **Power Automate** — used when clients are already on Microsoft 365.
- **Claude Code, ChatGPT, Gemini, Codex** — LLM engines for agent reasoning, content generation, and build assistance.
- **Python / JavaScript** — custom logic where no-code tools fall short.
- **REST APIs, Webhooks, JSON** — the integration backbone connecting CRM/ERP/SaaS systems.
- **Airtable, Notion, Supabase, Xano** — lightweight data layers and backends for client-specific tools.
- **Pipedrive, Monday, Hubspot, Odoo** — CRM/ERP systems configured and automated to fit each client's stack.
- **Jotform, Tally, Fillout** — form automation and intake handling.
