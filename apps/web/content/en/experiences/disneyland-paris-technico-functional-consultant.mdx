## Context

Disneyland Paris runs its website and mobile app (disneylandparis.com) on Tridion, a CMS shared across the Europe, Americas, and Asia entities of the Disney group. Each region has its own priorities and release calendar, which made Run/Build coordination and CMS governance the core of the role — both at the mobile app content layer and at the broader multi-region platform level.

## My Role & Responsibilities

**On mobile app CMS evolutions, as Functional Designer:**

- Gathered business and IT requirements for CMS evolutions on the Disneyland Paris mobile app.
- Ran workshops with business stakeholders and developers to identify content design needs.
- Wrote Enablers and technical-functional User Stories, mapping new fields and schemas in the CMS.
- Took part in Product Backlog Refinement, sprint planning, and retrospectives.
- Coordinated between development and QA teams.
- Tracked, assigned, and verified defects in JIRA.
- Trained integration teams on the app's different mobile screens.
- Designed new content schemas and fields directly in XML, and handled CMS configuration.
- Used REST APIs and Insomnia to validate and test CMS content delivery.
- Wrote reports for functional and technical sessions.

**At the platform level, owning Run, Build, and deployments across Disney's international entities:**

- Gathered business requirements, ran functional workshops, wrote functional specs and User Stories, and prioritized the shared backlog.
- Coordinated Product, Design, IT, and QA teams, plus the CMS vendor's developers, to drive feature evolutions and guarantee delivery quality.
- Tracked development, prepared releases, coordinated UAT, and managed defects via JIRA in an Agile/Scrum environment.
- Planned evolutions, estimated effort, ran risk analysis, and coordinated application migrations, supporting teams through transition phases.
- Defined processes and application governance, documented best practices, and supported users through deployments.
- Contributed to functional and technical scoping of new features, ran feasibility studies, and coordinated with international teams for consistent platform rollout.
- Acted as the owner of data consistency, content schemas, and publishing workflows across the shared platform.

## Team & Environment

Cross-functional coordination across Product, Design, IT, and QA teams, plus the CMS vendor's dev team, across three regions (Europe, Americas, Asia), working Agile/Scrum with JIRA and Confluence as the shared governance and documentation layer.

## Key Projects Delivered

- Shipped CMS content schema evolutions on the Disneyland Paris mobile app, from requirements gathering to XML schema design through CMS configuration and release.
- Owned Run and Build for the shared Tridion CMS platform across the three regional Disney entities.
- Coordinated application evolutions, migrations, and releases with reduced risk across multiple simultaneous regional roadmaps.
- Defined and rolled out application governance and documentation processes adopted platform-wide.

## KPIs & Results

- Maintained stable Run operations alongside active Build work across three regions for the full ~46-month engagement.
- Delivered releases and application migrations with reduced risk through structured risk analysis and phased transition planning.
- Kept content schemas and publishing workflows consistent on a platform shared by multiple international entities.

## Challenges

- Conflicting requirements across regional entities (Europe, Americas, Asia), each with its own priorities and release calendar, requiring negotiation and a shared, governed backlog rather than region-by-region decisions.
- Migration and release risk on a platform multiple teams depended on simultaneously — any misstep had cross-regional impact.
- Keeping Product, Design, IT, QA, and the CMS vendor's developers aligned on priorities and delivery quality across a fragmented, multi-region backlog.
- Translating business content requirements into precise XML schemas and field mappings the CMS could actually support, without breaking existing content structures.

## Tech Stack & Why Each Tool Was Chosen

- **Tridion CMS** — the group's shared content platform across regions; the role centered on configuring and governing it rather than choosing it.
- **XML** — used to design and map new content schemas and fields directly in the CMS.
- **REST API / Insomnia** — used to test and validate CMS content delivery to the mobile app.
- **JIRA** — backlog governance, defect tracking, and release coordination visible to every team and region.
- **Confluence** — shared documentation for functional specs, governance processes, and best practices.
- **Agile / Scrum** — ceremonies used to keep multi-region priorities in sync.
