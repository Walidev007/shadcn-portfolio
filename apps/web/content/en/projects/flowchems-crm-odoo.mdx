Deployed and configured an Odoo CRM to structure and automate the sales activity of a company specialized in industrial inspection.

## Context

Flowchems is a company specialized in industrial inspection.

Before the project, sales management ran mainly on scattered tools and ad hoc communication. Client requests, follow-ups, and opportunity tracking were handled manually, with no CRM or structured sales pipeline.

The main pain points were:

- requests tracked over email, WhatsApp, and phone
- no centralized sales pipeline
- manual opportunity tracking
- invoices and documents prepared one by one
- little visibility into sales activity
- no metrics to track performance

The business worked, but the sales process ran essentially on team habits and memory.

## Problem

The lack of a centralized system made it hard to:

- see open opportunities at a glance
- track requests and follow-ups
- identify the stage and value of each deal
- standardize sales documents
- steer the business on reliable data

The goal was to put in place a tool simple enough to actually use, capable of centralizing sales activity without imposing a process disconnected from how the team actually worked.

## My Role

I supported Flowchems in structuring and standing up its sales process.

My work covered:

- analyzing the existing way of working
- defining the sales pipeline
- deploying and configuring Odoo CRM
- structuring data around leads and opportunities
- setting up automations
- automating document generation
- onboarding users to the new tool

My role wasn't just to install a CRM — it was to translate a largely manual sales operation into a structured system usable day to day.

## Challenges Encountered

**Starting from a barely formalized process.** The client had no real documented sales process. That meant observing and understanding how the team actually worked before defining pipeline stages, the information to track, service types, opportunity qualification rules, and the data needed for sales tracking. The challenge was structuring the process without over-engineering it — a CRM can very quickly turn into a place where information gets stored that nobody wants to fill in.

**Centralizing multiple channels.** Requests could come in over email, WhatsApp, or phone. The risk was losing opportunities or failing to follow up consistently. That called for a central point where teams could track requests and opportunities in one place.

**Managing change.** The team was used to working mostly out of their inbox. Rolling out a CRM meant a shift in daily habits too. The goal was to build a system simple enough to actually get adopted.

## What Was Built

**1. Structuring the sales process.** Analyzed how requests came in and were handled, to build a sales pipeline that fit Flowchems' way of working. Opportunities were structured around stages (New → Qualified → Proposal → Won) and categorized by service type (QA/QC, mechanical inspection, electrical inspection, NDT, other inspection services). Additional fields were configured to track key opportunity information.

**2. Odoo CRM deployment and configuration.** Odoo was configured as the central system for managing sales activity, centralizing leads, contacts, opportunities, pipeline stages, deal value, and status. Teams now have a much clearer view of the pipeline and active deals.

**3. Process automation.** Set up automations with Make to cut down on manual work: pulling new requests into the CRM, updating opportunities, tracking certain pipeline stages, and handling follow-ups and related actions on deals. These automations reduce repetitive manual steps and the risk of a request getting lost in an inbox.

**4. Automated document generation.** Connected CRM data to Google Docs to automatically generate certain sales documents from information already stored in Odoo, instead of starting from a blank document each time. The goal was to cut manual entry, standardize documents, reduce copy errors, and speed up preparation.

**5. User onboarding.** Rollout was paired with hands-on onboarding to the new environment, so teams could use the pipeline and tools in their day-to-day work.

## Business Value Delivered

- **Centralized sales opportunities** in a single tool.
- **A structured view of the pipeline** and better tracking of deal progress.
- **Reduced reliance** on email and scattered communication.
- **Standardization** of key document preparation.
- **Automation** of a share of repetitive tasks.
- **A usable data foundation** to track and steer sales activity.

The result isn't just "a CRM got installed." It's a more structured sales process, with centralized information and a foundation that can grow with the business.

## Tech Stack

- **Odoo** — used as the central system for managing leads, contacts, and sales opportunities. Odoo made it possible to stand up a full, configurable CRM quickly, without building a custom solution.
- **Make** — used to connect different tools and automate workflows around requests, opportunities, and sales tracking.
- **Google Docs** — used to generate documents from CRM data and avoid manually rebuilding the same documents every time.
